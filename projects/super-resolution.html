4K Super-Resolution & VLM-Based Video Restoration

Alibaba Digital Media & Entertainment Group (Youku) | 2020 – Present

Overview

Led Youku's 4K super-resolution and video restoration initiative, supporting large-scale deployment across premium video enhancement services and restoration scenarios. Developed a VLM-based framework by adapting Wan T2V/I2V base models with LoRA fine-tuning for production-scale video restoration.

Technical Highlights

  • Developed a VLM-based video super-resolution and restoration framework by adapting Wan T2V/I2V base models with LoRA fine-tuning, including a single-step diffusion tailored for SR and restoration scenarios.
  • Built a LoRA training framework with PyTorch Accelerate for multi-GPU fine-tuning and experimentation, enabling efficient adaptation of large-scale generative visual models.
  • Introduced the Wan-Animate method with same-identity reference-based face replacement to address identity drift in face restoration and facial structure distortion under severe degradation.
  • Built hybrid degradation datasets by analyzing quality loss across shooting, post-production, compression, transcoding, and playback pipelines.
  • Productionized the VLM-based restoration service, integrating task-specific repair modules, lightweight models, and traditional image processing.
