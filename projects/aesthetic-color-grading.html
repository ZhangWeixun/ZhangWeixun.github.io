Aesthetic Color Grading

Alibaba Digital Media & Entertainment Group (Youku) | Technical Lead | 2020 – Present

Built an automated SDR aesthetic color grading system from scratch, modeling professional colorists' workflow to deliver cinematic-quality results at production scale — replacing costly manual grading with an efficient model-driven pipeline.

Technical Highlights

  • Dark Region Balancing: Extracted dark region masks to obtain per-channel RGB components, then balanced the three channels to eliminate color cast while enhancing shadow detail and depth — resulting in cleaner, more transparent images.
  • Skin Tone Correction: Built an adaptive skin tone correction model in HSV color space — constructed an empirical skin tone gamut, analyzed facial region centroids, and adaptively adjusted color mapping to ensure natural and aesthetically pleasing skin tone results.
  • Temporal Consistency: Introduced shot-level color consistency control to stabilize dark region and skin tone adjustments within the same shot, plus scene similarity estimation with inter-shot compensation to unify the grading effect across adjacent similar scenes, preventing flickering and visual inconsistency.
