Technical Highlights

  • Built an automated aesthetic color grading system modeling contrast, color richness, and color correction using image processing and cinematic grading principles.
  • Introduced shot-level two-pass analysis, temporal smoothing, and program-level outlier protection to preserve both visual consistency and artistic intent.
  • Built end-to-end support for HDR10, HLG, HDR Vivid, and Dolby Vision formats.
  • Developed an AI-assisted HDR remastering workflow integrated with professional grading tools.
  • Designed screen calibration and device certification systems to ensure consistent color and luminance across mobile, tablet, and TV devices.
  • Reduced cost to ~30% of fully manual workflows while improving production efficiency by ~3×.
