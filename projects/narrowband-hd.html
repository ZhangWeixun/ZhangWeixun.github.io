Narrowband HD Enhancement

Alibaba Digital Media & Entertainment Group (Youku) | 2020 – Present

Overview

Led the evolution of narrowband HD video enhancement, combining deep learning, traditional image processing, encoders, and players to improve visual quality under constrained bandwidth. The technology is deployed across live streaming and cloud video scenarios, supporting both UGC and PGC transcoding pipelines.

Technical Highlights

  • Designed a server-side denoising and sharpening pipeline with guided-filter-based processing, optimized via separable kernels and fixed-point quantization.
  • Introduced deep-learning-based denoising, deblocking, and enhancement into production transcoding services.
  • Implemented ROI-aware post-processing for perceptual fidelity, ensuring critical regions maintain visual quality.
  • Deployed across live streaming and cloud video scenarios, supporting adoption across UGC and PGC transcoding pipelines.
